**Status: proposed design.** Every consequential lesson should lead back to the experience that supports it.

## What a case contains

- The objective and instructions that applied at the time.
- The decisions and actions relevant to the outcome.
- The observed result and the user’s feedback.
- A stable pointer to the original transcript or artifact.
- The proposed explanation, including uncertainty and counterexamples.

## Evidence and interpretation

An agent finishing successfully does not prove every action helped. A failed result does not make every decision wrong. A useful case distinguishes what happened from the explanation proposed afterward.

[[knowledge-consolidation|Knowledge consolidation]] can produce concise lessons, but the underlying case remains available for review.

## Historical inspection

Superseded guidance should point to its replacement. Historical queries should be able to recover the former state and why it changed. [[../examples/revising-a-fact|Revising a fact]] illustrates this distinction.
