**Status: proposed design.** A specialist combines a small role prompt with relevant skills and knowledge sources.

## Example roles

| Role | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Implementer | Deliver a working change and verify the behavior that matters. |
| Reviewer | Inspect assumptions, evidence, and consequential defects. |
| Researcher | Find and assess primary sources; separate findings from inference. |
| Captain | Delegate substantial work, coordinate dependencies, and inspect results. |

These descriptions illustrate how profiles could differ. They do not create permissions or change any live agent.

## What belongs in the profile

Enduring responsibilities, decision priorities, and pointers to relevant skills. Current run numbers, temporary errors, and unfinished task lists belong in task state.

## Learning from experience

Review [[../memory/evidence-and-history|success and failure cases]], propose scoped changes, and retain revisions that improve later work. [[../research/memory-research|GEPA and ReasoningBank]] offer relevant approaches.

Use [[../memory/knowledge-consolidation|consolidation]] to keep reusable knowledge accessible without making every agent’s prompt contain the entire archive.
